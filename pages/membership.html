You keep all XP and Badges earned. Your account reverts to "Drifter" status (Free Flow), locking access to the Marketplace selling features and extra storage.

Not yet. We are currently integrating FlowTokens as a payment method for Phase 25. For now, we accept Stripe (Card) and PayPal.

Master Workspace members get physical access benefits at our partner locations (e.g., African Queen Kitchen, Outbreak Studio), including desk space booking and discounts.
