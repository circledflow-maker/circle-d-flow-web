Methodology

The Journey

Idea → Structure → Connection → Creation → Experience → Sharing

01 — FEEL

What is in your heart?

Project idea, motivation, cultural purpose, desired experience, audience.

02 — SHAPE

What does your idea need?

Type, objectives, location, timeline, scale, resources, budget.

03 — CONNECT

Who needs to be involved?

Artists, curators, venues, sponsors, production, community partners.

04 — BUILD

How do we make it happen?

Plan, timeline, tasks, budget, production, communication, marketing.

05 — EXPERIENCE

What should people experience?

Audience journey, atmosphere, program, execution, guest experience.

06 — SHARE

What remains after?

Documentation, visibility, community feedback, learnings, future opportunities.
