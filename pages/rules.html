The Constitution

To maintain the Flow, we must respect the boundaries of the Kingdom.

I. The Philosophy

We are building a "True Kingdom" — a digital space governed not by algorithms, but by respect, creativity, and shared value.

In Japanese, Ikigai means "reason for being." Here, our reason is to help each other ascend. Energy vampires, hate speech, and spam disrupt the frequency. They will not be tolerated.

warning II. The 3-Strike Rule

Our Guardians (moderators) and the Community Watch (you) monitor the realm. We operate on a strict 3-Strike Protocol.

1

Strike One: Warning

A formal warning is issued via your Dashboard. Behavior must be corrected immediately.

2

Strike Two: Suspension

Temporary freeze of Marketplace and Social privileges (24 - 72 hours).

3

Strike Three: Exile

Permanent account ban. Unlocking requires a direct appeal to the Game Master.

By entering the Kingdom, you agree to uphold these values.
