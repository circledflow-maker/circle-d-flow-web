Q & A

Is Bronze really free?

Yes. Claim your digital Member Card, get +25 EXP, Sanctuary and Orbit alerts. No card required.

Can I cancel Silver or Gold anytime?

Yes via Stripe. Paid perks end at period close. EXP and Bronze card remain.

What is Flowee?

Your guide in the Circle — language, registration, reminders for Next Flow, and help inside Membership and Admin.

Do you issue invoices / DATEV?

Stripe handles payment receipts. For German bookkeeping we keep invoice-ready records (customer, amount, VAT if applicable, period, Stripe ID) exportable for DATEV-compatible accounting on request.

Where is my data stored?

Account & profile in Supabase (EU-capable). Payments via Stripe. Cookies for language & card preference — you choose notifications separately.

Legal · Privacy · DATEV

Transparent membership terms aligned with EU GDPR and German accounting practice (DATEV-ready records).

  • Contract — Digital membership via Stripe. Controller: Circle D Flow / Wako Kungo community operators (Lisbon). Contact via Instagram @wako.kungo.
  • Right of withdrawal — For digital services started immediately after payment, you may waive the 14-day EU withdrawal where required; cancel anytime for the next period.
  • Prices & VAT — Shown prices are the charged amount. If VAT applies, it is itemized on Stripe receipts / invoices.
  • DATEV / bookkeeping — We retain: invoice number or Stripe payment ID, date, customer email/name, net/gross, currency, product (Silver/Gold), billing period. Export for Steuerberater/DATEV on request. Retention typically 10 years (German AO) for booking documents.
  • GDPR — Legal bases: contract (Art. 6(1)(b)), legitimate interest for security (Art. 6(1)(f)), consent for cookies/notifications (Art. 6(1)(a)). Rights: access, rectification, erasure, portability, complaint to a supervisory authority.
  • Fair use — Benefit QR codes are personal, one staff scan, refill next calendar month. Abuse may pause perks without deleting your card or EXP.
  • Processors — Stripe (payments), Supabase (auth/data), Vercel (hosting). No sale of personal data.
