## 🧭 The Vision

Circle D Flow is not just an app; it's a **Culture-Tech Ecosystem**. We transform the city of Lisbon into a living RPG (Role-Playing Game). By connecting local businesses (like the legendary *African Queen*) with a digital narrative, we create a sustainable economy where "Navigators" explore real-world locations to earn digital rewards and community status.

---

## 🛠 Tech Stack & Architecture

The "House" is built on a modern, scalable infrastructure:

* **Frontend:** Cinematic HTML5/CSS3 with a custom "Animus" Design-System.
* **Database & Auth:** [Supabase](https://supabase.com/) (PostgreSQL) for real-time data synchronization.
* **Mapping:** [Leaflet.js](https://leafletjs.com/) for a customized, interactive GPS-Atlas of Lisbon.
* **Game Logic:** Custom-built `Quest-Engine.js` for XP, Leveling, and Brotherhood ranking.

---

## 🏗 Project Structure

We follow a clean, modular architecture:

* `/assets`: Branding, Azulejo patterns, and Armillary textures.
* `/css`: Centralized style-sheets (Tech-Noir & Lisbon Gold themes).
* `/js`: Modular logic for Authentication, Map-Handling, and Quest-Engines.
* `/pages`: Specialized environments for the Map, the Board, and the Hall of Legends.
